## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the essence of contemporary artisanal refreshment: crisp, sunny, sun-drenched, yet grounded in deliberate editorial restraint. The personality balances breezy leisure with premium craft precision. Designed for discerning lifestyle consumers and modern beverage enthusiasts who appreciate transparency, botanical heritage, and unpretentious luxury, the visual tone evokes relaxed golden-hour gatherings, sensory effervescence, and clean indulgence.

The visual style merges modern editorial minimalism with subtle warmth:
- **Atmospheric Palette:** Soft washes of sunlit peach and tactile cream ivory replace sterile stark whites.
- **Typographic Poise:** High-contrast pairing of structural, geometric sans headlines with hyper-legible neutral body copy creates the pacing of an indie lifestyle publication.
- **Craft Detailing:** Grounded by authoritative deep nautical navy and subtle botanical green accents, avoiding over-sweetness while elevating product authority and craft heritage.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration directly from ripe stone fruit skins, artisanal can lithography, and crisp carbonation:

- **Primary (`#17263D` - Deep Navy):** The structural anchor used for primary calls to action, prominent navigation links, and primary typographic weight. It provides punchy contrast against light backgrounds.
- **Secondary (`#F5A982` - Warm Sunlit Peach):** The brand’s hero hue, delivering warmth and flavor cueing across badges, accent ribbons, hero highlights, and interactive states.
- **Tertiary (`#425448` - Botanical Green):** Derived from orchard foliage and botanical stems; reserved for herbal tags, secondary actions, sustainability badges, and organic certification stamps.
- **Neutral (`#F7F0E5` - Cream Ivory):** The atmospheric canvas tone. Replaces default whites to give the digital surface a textured, tactile paper quality.
- **Supporting Accents:**
  - Soft Tint Peach (`#F8C3A7`): Used for muted surface containers and hovered pill tags.
  - Muted Terracotta Coral (`#D98773`): Used for warnings, interactive hover states on navy buttons, and warm iconography.
  - Warm Pure White (`#FFFAF3`): Card surfaces and floating interactive controls to pop subtly off the ivory canvas.

## Typography

The typographic rhythm balances editorial presence with utilitarian e-commerce clarity:

- **Headlines (DM Sans):** Geometric, understated, and sculptural. Generates a premium editorial masthead feel with tight letter tracking on large headings. Display and headline levels should often be set in uppercase for badge micro-headers and flavor titles, or sentence case with confident brevity for campaign statements.
- **Body & Product Metadata (Inter):** Neutral, engineered, and exceptionally legible across digital screens. Used for nutritional facts, tasting notes, and transactional UI.
- **Micro-Copy & Eyebrows (DM Sans):** Capitalized, slightly tracked labels (`letter-spacing: 0.06em - 0.08em`) used for flavor tags, ABV percentages, and category markers.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a flexible 12-column grid on desktop/tablet viewports and a 4-column system on mobile devices. 

- **Density & Breathing Room:** Generous vertical rhythm reinforces the editorial magazine aesthetic. Hero sections and pack showcases utilize high vertical padding (incorporating `space-xl` multiples) to allow photography of liquid splashes and tactile packaging to breathe.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Mobile` (< 768px): 4 columns, `1rem` gutter, `1.25rem` margin.
  - `Tablet` (768px - 1024px): 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutter, `2rem` margin.
  - `Desktop` (> 1024px): 12 columns, max container width `1280px`, `1.5rem` gutter, `3rem` margin.
- **Asymmetric Spreads:** Product feature stories alternate between 7-column imagery spreads and 5-column editorial detail blocks to emulate art-directed magazine features.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained primarily through **tonal layering** and **ambient warmth shadows**, eschewing harsh digital drop shadows:

- **Canvas Tiering:** The baseline background rests on cream ivory (`#F7F0E5`). Content blocks, cards, and modal sheets elevate to warm pure white (`#FFFAF3`) or light peach containers (`#F8C3A7` at 25% opacity).
- **Ambient Shadows:** Elevation is tactile and soft, mimicking gentle sunlight through condensation. Shadows use deep navy tinting with high blur and low opacity:
  - *Card / Floating Pill:* `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(23, 38, 61, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(23, 38, 61, 0.03)`
  - *Hover Elevation:* `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(23, 38, 61, 0.10), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(23, 38, 61, 0.04)`
- **Ghost Borders:** Interactive borders and dividers employ faint peach-navy borders (`rgba(23, 38, 61, 0.08)`) to structure content without introducing hard lines.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a refined, architectural curvature (`roundedness: 1`, base `0.25rem` / 4px to `0.5rem` / 8px). This subtle rounding mirrors modern label die-cuts and vintage tin beverage can seams rather than playful software bubbles:

- **Buttons & Form Controls:** `0.25rem` (4px) border radius for a crisp, tailored architectural profile.
- **Cards & Visual Containers:** `0.5rem` (8px) for packaging cards and recipe containers.
- **Flavor & Metric Badges (Chips):** Full capsule pill shapes (`9999px`) to create intentional contrast against the geometric cards and rectangular hero images.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background deep navy (`#17263D`), text warm white (`#FFFAF3`), radius `0.25rem`. On hover: background shifts toward muted terracotta (`#D98773`) or deep indigo tint (`#101C2F`) with a subtle `2px` upward translation.
- **Secondary / Craft Accent:** Background warm peach (`#F5A982`), text deep navy (`#17263D`), border none.
- **Ghost / Editorial:** Transparent background with a `1px` border of deep navy (`#17263D`) and matching navy text.

### Chips & Flavor Tags
- Rounded capsule shape (`9999px`), uppercase `label-sm` DM Sans typography with `0.08em` letter spacing.
- Botanical green variant: Background `#425448`, text `#FFFAF3` for ingredients and certifications.
- Peach soda variant: Background `#F8C3A7`, text `#17263D` for tasting profiles ("Real Peach Puree", "Gluten Free", "100 Cal").

### Cards (Product & Editorial)
- Base background of warm white (`#FFFAF3`) sitting atop the cream ivory (`#F7F0E5`) page canvas.
- Crisp `1px` border using `rgba(23, 38, 61, 0.06)` combined with soft ambient navy shadows.
- Inner padding set to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`). Product pack shots overflow slightly at the top to break the container grid organically.

### Input Fields
- Understated input boxes with `#FFFAF3` backgrounds, `0.25rem` corner radii, and a `1px` border of `rgba(23, 38, 61, 0.15)`.
- Active focus state: `1.5px` border in deep navy (`#17263D`) accompanied by a subtle `#F5A982` (peach) glow ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(245, 169, 130, 0.35)`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Crisp `0.25rem` corner radius on checkboxes; pure circle on radios.
- Inactive state: `1.5px` stroke in `rgba(23, 38, 61, 0.25)`.
- Active state: Solid `#17263D` fill with `#FFFAF3` checkmark icon or radio dot.

### Specialized Lifestyle Components
- **Tasting Notes Barometer:** Horizontal meter indicating Peach Intensity, Dryness, and Effervescence using thin botanical green tracks and warm peach fill indicators.
- **Artisanal Story Banner:** Multi-column text section featuring drop caps in DM Sans and inline ingredient botanical illustrations.
