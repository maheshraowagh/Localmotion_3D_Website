Frequently Asked Questions

GOOD TO KNOW.

Local Motion is a modern, premium ready-to-drink beverage company dedicated to crafting clean, authentic vodka sodas made with pure ingredients, fine carbonation, and vibrant fruit character.
Our signature inaugural expression is Peach Vodka Soda—a balanced union of natural ripe peach profile, smooth craft vodka, and sparkling water. Additional seasonal flavours are currently in small-batch development.
Vodka soda is a classic highball cocktail pairing premium neutral grain spirit with crisp carbonated water. Local Motion elevates this timeless combination with real fruit notes and fine effervescence in a convenient canned format.
Local Motion is distributed at independent fine liquor retailers, select grocers, and premier hospitality venues. Use our interactive Store Locator above to find the nearest stocking location.
Our formula is simple and transparent: Ultra-pure Distilled Vodka, Carbonated Water, and Natural Peach Flavour. It contains 5% ALC/VOL and is naturally gluten-free.
We champion mindful enjoyment. You can review our responsible drinking guidelines below or visit national resources such as Responsibility.org for guidance on safe and moderate alcohol consumption.
